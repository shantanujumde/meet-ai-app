Brand-tinted surfaces

The window surfaces are not plain grey. Each is a neutral nudged a few percent toward the brand: Dusk coral in light, Dusk violet in dark. The sidebar is see-through glass over the native material, shown here over a mid grey and a busy backdrop so you can judge it where it is weakest. Hex values follow each swatch.
