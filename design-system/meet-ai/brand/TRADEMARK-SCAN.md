# meet-ai — trademark and prior-art desk scan for "m."

**Scope.** The app icon adopted on 2026-09-30 (README §1, §10): a heavy,
rounded, lower-case white **m** followed by a round full stop, on a rounded-square
tile with a vertical gradient from coral `#F46A3A` to violet `#9B3CF2`. The
product is an AI meeting-notes app for macOS, so the fields that matter are
Nice class 9 (downloadable software) and class 42 (software as a service). This
scan is about the **symbol**. It is not a search of the name "meet-ai"; there is
one line on the name at the end of the method section, and that is all.

**Date:** 2026-09-30.

**This is not legal advice and it is not a clearance.** It is a desk search by
the design team, done to catch obvious conflicts before the icon spreads. A
registered-mark clearance by counsel is still needed before a public launch.
Nothing here says the mark is free to use or to register.

## 1. Method and sources

| Source | Reached? | What was done |
|---|---|---|
| **USPTO** Trademark Search (tmsearch.uspto.gov) | **Yes** | Queried the search system's own public back end (the one its web page calls), live records only. Searches: mark descriptions with the letter m plus a period or dot; literal element `M` or `M.` in classes 9/42; lower-case m on a square, tile or background; m on orange/red-to-purple/violet; named owners (Xiaomi, monday.com, Meetup, Mem Labs, Instagram, Circleback, Memoth). Drawings of every close hit were pulled from TSDR and looked at, not just read about. |
| **EUIPO** eSearch / TMview | **No** | The eSearch page loads, but the search is a script-only app, and the TMview search API did not answer from here. No EU register search was run. |
| **WIPO** Global Brand Database | **No** | Behind a CAPTCHA. We do not get around CAPTCHAs, so no search was run. Madrid registrations that also reach the US (serials starting `79`) turn up in the USPTO results, which covers a little of the international picture, but not much. |
| **App Store** (US storefront, iPhone and Mac) | **Yes** | The iTunes Search API, about 60 queries: the meeting-notes field ("meeting notes ai", "ai note taker", "meeting recorder", "meeting minutes") and every brand named in the brief. About 400 apps. Every icon was rendered onto contact sheets and checked by eye for a white m, a letter plus a full stop, or a coral-to-violet tile. |
| **Web search** | Partly | Text search only: brand histories, owners, product descriptions. **No reverse image search was available** (no Google Lens or TinEye), and the browser extension was not connected, so there was no image-by-image search on the open web. |

**Limits worth knowing.** A USPTO mark description is free text written by an
examiner, so a mark described in other words can be missed. The broad "letter M
in class 9/42" set has **2,811 live records**; the top 150 by relevance were
read, and targeted keyword queries were run over the whole set. Unregistered
use outside the App Store (websites, Android, China) is barely covered. China,
the UK and every other national register were not searched.

**On the name, briefly:** one live USPTO record matches the name exactly —
`MEETAI`, reg. 6820650, Dongguan Panyun Network Technology, **class 28**
(toys and games), so a different field. Longer marks that contain "meet ai"
were not searched. This is not a name search either.

## 2. Findings

Risk is how likely it is to cause trouble for **using** the icon in software, or
for **registering** it in classes 9/42: **low**, **medium** or **high**. "Reg."
is a US registration number; "SN" is a US serial number for a filing that is not
yet registered. Each drawing can be seen at
`https://tsdr.uspto.gov/img/<serial>/large`.

## 3. Overall assessment

**Overall risk: medium.** No identical mark turned up, and nothing looks like a
clear block. Two things keep the rating above low.

1. **"m." is a crowded idea on the US register.** At least six live records use
   the literal element `M.` or `m.`, four of them in class 9 or 42, and
   monday.com owns a registered device (with and without colour) that the market
   reads as "m.". In practice that means:
   - **Use:** the risk comes mainly from **monday.com**. It is a large, active
     registrant in exactly our classes, and one of its own apps already shows a
     white "m." on a violet-family gradient. Our strongest points are the
     letterform (a real arched m with three legs, not two slashes) and the
     colour (coral to violet, vertical).
   - **Registration:** an application for the bare "m." in 9/42 should expect
     citations: monday.com, Memletz (whose letterform is closest), MarketWise,
     Motivated. Whatever protection we get will be **narrow**, tied to the drawn
     letterform, the tile and the Dusk colours together, not to "m." as an idea.
2. **A direct competitor has a similar idea.** Memoth, a Mac AI note-taker,
   uses an M with a baseline dot. It looks different, but a buyer comparing Mac
   meeting recorders will see two "m-dot" icons.

What is already working for us: the **dot on the baseline** (README §6) keeps
clear of Xiaomi's "MI" and Mistral's dotted m. The **two-stop vertical Dusk**
avoids Instagram's registered multi-stop sweep and monday's pink-to-blue
diagonal. The **heavy arched m** is visibly not monday's pair of slashes.

**One correction for README §9:** it calls Xiaomi's white "mi" on orange the
company's *old* logo, from before 2021. Xiaomi's 2021 redesign kept the orange
tile and the white "MI" and only rounded the corners, so it is the current mark
and still enforced. That does not change the risk here, because our dot is a
full stop, but README should not call the mark retired.

## 4. What to do next

1. **Clearance by counsel before any public launch.** Ask for a full search of
   the US, EU (EUIPO) and UK registers, plus Madrid, and China if we ship there,
   covering the device in classes 9 and 42 and the name "meet-ai". Give counsel
   this list to start from: monday.com regs. 5730508 and 8190928, Memletz reg.
   6825844, MarketWise reg. 8345874, Motivated reg. 7553719, Meitu reg.
   6599789, Instagram reg. 6104619.
2. **Run the searches this scan could not reach:** EUIPO eSearch, WIPO Global
   Brand Database and UKIPO by hand in a browser, and a reverse image search
   (Google Lens, TinEye) of `proofs/proof-dock-real-mdot-zoom.png` and the
   1024 master.
3. **If we file, file the composite**: the drawn m, the baseline dot, the
   rounded tile and a Dusk colour claim together, not "m." in plain letters or
   the glyph alone. Ask counsel whether to file the lockup, "m." with the
   `meet-ai` wordmark, as well.
4. **Keep the distance we already have, and write it into the rules.** Beyond
   README §6 (do not move the dot, do not recolour the tile):
   - never draw the m as slanted bars or strokes without arches;
   - never put the white glyph on a pink-to-blue or diagonal gradient;
   - never add a second small mark (a plus, a sparkle, a second dot).
5. **Pair the symbol with the name** in marketing, the App Store listing and
   onboarding wherever there is room, so "m." is always seen next to "meet-ai"
   and not next to monday.
6. **Keep watching Memoth** and new App Store entries in "AI meeting notes"; if
   the field fills up with m-dot icons, look at this again.
7. **Fix the Xiaomi wording in README §9** (above) the next time README is
   edited.
